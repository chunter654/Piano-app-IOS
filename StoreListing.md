# App Store listing

The text of the store listing, kept here rather than only in App Store
Connect.

Two reasons. The licence on the piano sound obliges us to credit it wherever
the app is distributed, and a credit that lives only in a web form is one
nobody can check. And the listing is deliberately short: a paid piano app is
bought by someone who wants a piano, not a feature list, so everything that is
not the promise, the two views, or the credit has been left out. Keeping it
here makes that a decision rather than an oversight.

The app is priced at $1.99 with nothing to buy inside it.

Field limits are Apple's. `Tools/check-listing.sh` measures them and fails if
the credit goes missing.

## Description (4000)

A piano app with no ads, no pop-ups, and the ability to play alongside your
music or any other app.

Features

Two piano views
Wide — 88 keys with adjustable key size; pinch the control bar to zoom.
Vertical — two octaves with simple octave navigation.

Piano sound: Salamander Grand Piano by Alexander Holm, licensed under Creative
